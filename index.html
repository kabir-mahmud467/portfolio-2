My Process

🧠

1. Deep Discovery

Before writing a single line of code, I dive into the core of your business. We map out the user journey, define strict technical requirements, and establish a solid architectural blueprint ensuring long-term scalability.

✨

2. UI/UX Design

I craft pixel-perfect, high-fidelity prototypes. Every element is designed with a premium Glassmorphism aesthetic, modern typography, and an intuitive user flow that guarantees maximum conversion.

⚡

3. Agile Development

Using React, Next.js, and Node.js, I build out the application in rapid sprints. Code is modular, rigorously tested, and fully optimized for blazing-fast performance and seamless SEO integration.

Deployment isn't an afterthought. I configure automated CI/CD pipelines, containerize with Docker, and deploy across global CDNs like AWS and Vercel for guaranteed 99.99% uptime and security.

Frequently Asked Questions

What technologies do you specialize in? +
I specialize in full-stack JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystems, primarily React, Next.js, Node.js, and NestJS. I also have deep expertise in Python, Django, and modern CSS techniques like Glassmorphism and Tailwind.
Are you available for freelance work? +
Yes! I take on selected freelance projects that excite me. Feel free to reach out via the contact page to discuss your project requirements.
How do you ensure high performance on your sites? +
I follow strict performance budgets, utilizing code-splitting, lazy loading, optimized asset delivery via CDNs, and efficient rendering strategies (SSR/SSG) with frameworks like Next.js or Astro.
